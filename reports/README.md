# Compliance reports

`minor-student-compliance-audit-2026-08-30.docx` and `report-source.md` record the pre-remediation product audit completed on August 30, 2026. They intentionally preserve the risks observed before the family co-learning release.

The same-day family release removed advertising and public rankings, replaced public anonymous feedback with a signed-in parent channel and prior review, moved account ownership to the adult parent or legal guardian, added versioned consent, added category deletion, and implemented four-month family-data and six-month account retention. Those changes reduce the audit findings but do not constitute a legal certification. The report's remaining recommendations—especially jurisdiction-specific lawful basis, verifiable parental consent where required, operator contact and rights operations, school-use controls, DPIA/children's assessment, and local counsel review—remain open.
